SCNSK brand kit (Skincare & Skin Talk, said "Skin Talk")

svg/    Master vector files: outlined paths, no fonts needed. Use these for print, merch and web.
png/    Transparent exports: avatars (1080), favicon (32), apple-touch-icon (180), stamps (2000), logos (2400).
fonts/  Archivo and Archivo Expanded (variable woff2, SIL Open Font License). On the web, load Archivo
        from Google Fonts: family=Archivo:wdth,wght@62..125,100..900 and use font-stretch:125% for Expanded.

Colors
  Forest #1E3A2F  ink
  Moss   #4E6B4F  secondary text
  Sage   #A9BCA0  color blocks
  Mist   #DDE5D4  soft fields
  Oat    #F1ECE0  warm ground
  Paper  #FAF7F0  page
  Clay   #A94D2C  the one accent. Use it once per view.

Rules
  - Clay appears once per logo.
  - The Talk Drop's square corner always sits bottom-left.
  - On sage, use the -forest logo files.

brand-book/ has the manifesto, voice guide, logo rules, visual rules, and design tokens (tokens.json).
The live version, with component previews, is the SCNSK design system.
