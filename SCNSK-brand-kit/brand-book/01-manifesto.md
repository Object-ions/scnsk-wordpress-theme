# Manifesto

**Skincare got loud.**

Every week brings a new miracle, a new twelve-step routine, and a new ingredient you're told you can't live without. It's everywhere on Monday and gone by Friday. Somewhere in all that noise, the people who just want better skin get lost.

I know that noise from the inside. I've spent ten years in the skincare industry and tried more products than I'd like to admit. That's where the name comes from: I'm the Skincare Junkie. **I tried it all so you don't have to.**

All that trying taught me one thing. Most of it doesn't matter, and a few things matter a lot.

**SCNSK** (Skincare & Skin Talk, said "Skin Talk") is where I tell you which is which. I say it straight, with no hype, no hard sell, and no ten-dollar words when a plain one works.

## What we stand for

1. **Skin over trends.** Your skin doesn't care what went viral.
2. **Less, done right.** A routine that works fits on one hand.
3. **Always the why.** If I can't explain it simply, I won't recommend it.
4. **Beginners welcome.** Nobody's born knowing this. You're not behind, you're early.
5. **Honest verdicts.** Sometimes the answer is "you don't need it," and when it is, I'll say so.

> Hype is loud. Good skin is quiet.
>
> **No hype. Just skin.**
>
> — Skincare Junkie

## Positioning

For people just getting into skincare who are drowning in hype, **SCNSK** is the straight-talking skin blog that tells you what actually works, what doesn't, and why. It's written by a reformed Skincare Junkie who's tried it all so you don't have to.
