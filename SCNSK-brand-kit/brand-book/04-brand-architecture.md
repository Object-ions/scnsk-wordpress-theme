# Brand architecture

SCNSK is the house, and everything else lives under it with one clear name each.

| Layer | Name | How it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **Masterbrand** | **SCNSK** · Skincare & Skin Talk | Written SCNSK, said "Skin Talk." The stacked logo is the primary. |
| **Author** | **Skincare Junkie** | Byline "by Skincare Junkie" and the sign-off *I tried it all so you don't have to.* Always paired with SCNSK in public ("Skincare Junkie · SCNSK") so the alias stays findable. |
| **Blog** | skincareandskintalk.com | The wordmark in the header and the monogram as favicon. Posts follow the post anatomy in Voice. |
| **Book** | *The Perfect Skincare Routine* | "An SCNSK book by Skincare Junkie." Title in `display-xl` Archivo Expanded, Bloom on the spine, stamp on the back cover. |
| **Video & social** | **Skin Talk** | The show name for Reels, TikTok and a future podcast: "Skin Talk with Skincare Junkie." The avatar is `scnsk-avatar-sage` and the handle target is `@scnsk`. |
| **Merch & products** | SCNSK | Stamp-led: stickers, totes, pouches. Products carry the stacked logo on the front and the stamp as a seal. |

## Naming rules

- New series get plain-English names that sit under "Skin Talk" or SCNSK, like "Skin Talk: Ingredient Basics." Don't invent new coined words.
- Always pair "Skincare Junkie" with SCNSK. It's a common phrase and won't be ownable on its own.
- "Skin Talk" is the spoken name and the show name. It never replaces SCNSK in the logo.

## Before you commit

- Check handle availability for `@scnsk` on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Threads, and reserve the "Skin Talk" show name variants.
- A skincare forum already operates as "Skin Care Talk" at skincaretalk.com. That's why SCNSK, not the spelled-out name, is the mark.
