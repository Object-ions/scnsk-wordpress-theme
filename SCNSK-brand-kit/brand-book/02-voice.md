# Voice

SCNSK sounds like **a friend who actually knows skin**. That friend is straight-talking, warm underneath and never selling. The reader is a curious beginner, so every post leaves them less confused than it found them.

## Rules

1. **Lead with the answer, then give the why.** The first line of every post answers the headline's question.
2. **Use short sentences and contractions.** Write "you'll," not "you will." Read it aloud; if you wouldn't say it, rewrite it.
3. **Translate every term the first time it appears.** For example: "niacinamide (a form of vitamin B3)" or "your skin barrier (the outer layer that keeps water in)."
4. **Call hype hype, and don't sneer at the reader.** Criticize the claim, not the person who bought it.
5. **Say "you don't need this" when it's true.** It's the most on-brand sentence we have.
6. **Write as "I" and "you."** Skincare Junkie writes in first person, and site chrome and the About page speak as SCNSK.
7. **Use sentence case everywhere.** SCNSK is always in capitals, "Skincare" is one word, and "Skin Talk" is title case when it names the show.
8. **Use at most one exclamation mark per post.** Don't put emoji in headlines or graphics.

## Say / don't say

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| This works for most people. Here's why. | This miracle serum will transform your skin! |
| You don't need this. | Must-have! Holy grail! |
| Sunscreen, every day. That's most of it. | The secret to glass skin overnight |
| It's fine, but a $12 one does the same job. | Luxury you deserve |
| Retinol (a vitamin A that speeds up cell turnover) | Retinol's cell-regenerating anti-aging power |
| Give it 8 weeks before you judge it. | Instant results! |
| I tried it. It's okay. | Obsessed!! 😍 |

## Banned words

miracle · holy grail · game-changer · must-have · flawless · detox · anti-aging secret · glass skin overnight · chemical-free · clean (as a claim) · clinically proven (unless you link the study)

## Post anatomy

1. **Headline:** the reader's real question, in sentence case, set in `display-l`.
2. **Lead:** the straight answer in two sentences, set in `lead`.
3. **The why:** plain explanation with every term translated.
4. **The verdict:** one `body-strong` line, the HypeCheck component, or both.
5. **Sign-off:** a Talk Drop end mark, then *— Skincare Junkie*.
