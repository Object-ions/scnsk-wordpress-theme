# Logo system

The logos come in five forms, and each has one job. All of them are outlined vector paths (no live fonts), drawn from Archivo Expanded Black and the Talk Drop.

| Form | File family | Job | Minimum size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stacked logo** (primary) | `scnsk-logo-stacked-*` | First impressions, About page, book cover, press | 140px / 35mm wide |
| **Wordmark** | `scnsk-wordmark-*` | Site header, banners, video end cards | 96px / 25mm wide |
| **Horizontal lockup** | `scnsk-logo-horizontal-*` | Email header, lower-thirds, anywhere a symbol helps | 120px / 30mm wide |
| **Monogram** (SK in the Talk Drop) | `scnsk-monogram-*`, `scnsk-avatar-*`, `scnsk-favicon.svg` | Social avatars, favicon, app icon, photo watermark | 24px / 8mm |
| **Stamp** | `scnsk-stamp-*` | Merch, packaging, stickers, book back cover | 160px / 32mm diameter |

## How the name is built

- **SC · N · SK** stands for **S**kin**C**are **'n' SK**in. The clay N is the "and," which is why it's the one letter that changes color.
- **SK in a speech bubble** means "Skin Talk," which is how the brand is spoken.
- The **Talk Drop** is a drop of product and a speech bubble at once.

## Rules

1. **Clay appears once per logo.** The wordmark's N is clay. In the horizontal lockup the Talk Drop is clay, so the N goes to ink.
2. **Clear space** is half the wordmark's cap height on every side. For the monogram, it's a quarter of its width.
3. **Pick the version by the ground:**
   - On paper, oat or mist, use `-color` or `-forest`.
   - On forest, use `-oat` (clay N kept) or `-oat-mono`.
   - On sage, use `-forest` only. The clay N vibrates on sage.
   - On photos, use the mono version with the most contrast, or put the monogram in its own solid Talk Drop.
4. **The Talk Drop's square corner always sits bottom-left.** Never rotate or mirror the monogram. That corner is the speech tail.
5. **Merch, embroidery, screen print and single-color stamps** use the one-color files: `-forest`, `-clay`, `-oat` and `scnsk-monogram-knockout-forest.svg`, where the letters are cut through to the garment.

## Don'ts

- Don't retype SCNSK in any font. Use the files.
- Don't stretch, condense, outline, add shadows or add gradients.
- Don't color the N anything other than clay or the logo's own ink.
- Don't write "Scnsk," "S.C.N.S.K." or "SCN SK."
- Don't put the full-color logo on sage or on a busy photo.
- Don't use the Bloom as a logo. It's a flourish and pattern, never a sign-off for the brand.

## The Bloom and the pattern

The **Bloom** is four Talk Drops with their square corners meeting, which makes a four-petal flower. Use it on merch, as section dividers, on the book's spine, and at big scale cropped off a color block. `scnsk-pattern-tile.svg` repeats sage Blooms and forest Talk Drops on mist. Use it for wrapping, tissue paper, a tote lining and social backgrounds, and never behind body text.
